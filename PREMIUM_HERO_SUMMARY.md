## 🐛 Known Limitations

### Browser Support
- Modern browsers only (Chrome 88+, Firefox 87+, Safari 14+)
- Requires backdrop-filter support
- No IE11 support

### Performance
- Best on desktop/tablet
- May be heavy on older mobile devices
- Consider reducing agents on slow devices
