✦ How it Works

From idea to automation in 3 steps

No engineering degree required. If you can describe what you want, FlowMitra can automate it.

1

Choose a trigger

Pick what starts your flow: a new email, form submission, scheduled time, or webhook from any app.

2

Add AI & actions

Drop in AI nodes to process data, then connect actions to send messages, update databases, or call APIs.

3

Deploy & monitor

Activate your flow with one click. Watch it run in real-time and get alerts if anything needs attention.

4

Iterate & scale

Improve your flows with live data. Clone, version, and share automations across your entire team.
